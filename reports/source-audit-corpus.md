# Corpus source-identity handoff (2026-09-22)

The 64-paper corpus contains **332 canonical candidate group IDs** with paper evidence, after **6 evidence-confirmed alias redirects** from 338 original proposals. This audit contains **157 source investigations** covering **156 canonical groups**. The [structured ledger](../work/sources/source-audit-corpus.json) retains every original mention receipt and explicit alias mapping. Source research does not accept a dataset release, approve public media, or prove an adapter.

Coverage ledger: **156** canonical groups researched here; **20** in the [publication audit](source-audit-publication.md); **18** exact candidates plus **2** related variants in the [initial jobs audit](source-audit-jobs.md); **40** in [jobs batch 2](../work/sources/source-audit-jobs-batch2.json); **25** in [jobs batch 3](../work/sources/source-audit-jobs-batch3.json); **23** in [publication batch 2](../work/sources/source-audit-publication.json); **34** in [publication batch 3](../work/sources/source-audit-publication.json); **14** in existing registry owner source receipts (file paths recorded per candidate); **0** still pending original-source work. These count identity proposals, not distinct accepted datasets.

Two redirects are same-paper duplicate names (the 40-instruction set and JailBreakV_28K); four are source-verified CIFAR/VQA name spellings across papers. All page receipts and paper-specific roles remain attached to their canonical candidate IDs; name aliases do not establish identical paper-used splits. **3** confirmed method/text-extraction candidates are excluded from the dataset catalogue; [the exclusion manifest](../work/corpus/excluded_dataset_candidates.json) retains their paper and source receipts.
