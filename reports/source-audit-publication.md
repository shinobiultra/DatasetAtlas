# Direct-use benchmark source audit (2026-09-22)

This is a source-identity and access handoff, **not human review or publication approval**. It covers 77 named source/protocol audits: an initial 19 direct-use groups, one RobustBench identity correction, 23 additional named groups, and 34 pending canonical groups. Some are only survey or bibliography mentions, not direct-use datasets. The structured evidence and adapter actions are in [`work/sources/source-audit-publication.json`](../work/sources/source-audit-publication.json). No corpus original, source archive, or model was changed. A separate metadata-only synchronization updated 16 registry entries from the second batch; it did not add dataset content or claim review. Official project pages, author repositories, NIST/Zenodo records, and author-linked hosted files were checked. Small HTTP HEAD or one-byte Range requests measured sizes; the listed datasets were not acquired or parsed in this audit.
